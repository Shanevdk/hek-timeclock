QuickBooks connection

Each pay period the hours below are pushed into QuickBooks so payroll opens with them already filled in. QuickBooks has no way to let an app run payroll or send direct deposit, so the final Run payroll click stays with you.

Intuit app

From your app at developer.intuit.com → My Apps → Keys & credentials. Sandbox and production have separate keys — make sure these match the environment picked below.

Pay schedule

Pick any pay period's first day — every other period is counted from it.

Overtime thresholds are a labour-law question, so nothing is assumed — 44 hours/week is standard in Ontario, 40 under US federal rules. Leave it at 0 to send every hour as regular time.

With both payroll item ids set, each day goes over as two entries — regular and overtime — tagged with those items. Left blank, each day is one entry for the day's total and the split is written into its description.

Invoices

QuickBooks needs every invoice line billed against one of its products or services. Pick the one your fencing work belongs to — your own wording still goes across as the line description.

Tax is left to QuickBooks. Our invoices carry a flat percentage; QuickBooks works it out from the tax code and the customer, and QuickBooks is what files the return. If the two totals disagree, you are told rather than left to find out later.
